Overview

The CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 is the current vendor-neutral entry-level security certification. Here are consolidated notes across all exam domains.

Domain 1: General Security Concepts

Covers fundamental security controls, cryptography basics, and common threat types.

  • CIA Triad — Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
  • AAA — Authentication, Authorization, Accounting
  • Symmetric vs. Asymmetric encryption
  • PKI and certificate management
  • Zero Trust — never trust, always verify

Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations

Threat Actor Types:
  Nation-state  → Advanced, well-funded, targeted (APT)
  Cybercriminal → Financially motivated
  Hacktivist    → Ideologically driven
  Insider       → Most dangerous — has trusted access

Domain 3: Security Architecture

Cloud models (IaaS/PaaS/SaaS), on-prem vs. hybrid, network segmentation, DMZ, VLAN.

Domain 4: Security Operations

SIEM, EDR, vulnerability scanning (Nessus, OpenVAS), patch management lifecycle.

Domain 5: Security Program Management

Risk management frameworks (NIST RMF, ISO 27001), compliance, data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA).

Exam Tips

  • Focus on the why behind controls — exam is scenario-heavy
  • Jason Dion's practice exams are closest to the real thing
  • PBQ (performance-based questions) appear first — don't spend too long on them
  • 90 questions, 90 minutes — pace yourself at 1 min/question
  • Know your port numbers cold: 22 SSH, 25 SMTP, 53 DNS, 443 HTTPS, 3389 RDP
